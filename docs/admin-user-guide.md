# Admin User Guide

This guide is for the YJM BOY shop owner or staff.

## Login

1. Open `/admin/login`.
2. Enter your admin email and password.
3. Click `Login`.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows:

- Total enquiries
- New enquiries
- Contacted enquiries
- Closed enquiries
- Total products
- Low stock items

Use this page to quickly see what needs attention.

## Add A Product

1. Open `Products`.
2. Click `Add Product`.
3. Enter:
   - Product name
   - Category
   - Price
   - Stock quantity
   - Description
   - Product image
4. Tick `Available for customers` if it should show on the public website.
5. Click `Create Product`.

## Edit A Product

1. Open `Products`.
2. Find the product.
3. Click `Edit`.
4. Change the details.
5. Click `Save Product`.

## Product Images

- Use JPG, PNG, or WEBP images.
- Maximum image size is 2MB.
- The image preview shows before saving.

## Stock Updates

On the Products page:

- Click `+` to increase stock by 1.
- Click `-` to decrease stock by 1.
- Stock will not go below 0.

## Hide Or Show Products

- Click `Hide` to remove a product from the public website.
- Click `Show` to make it public again.

## Enquiries

1. Open `Enquiries`.
2. Search by customer name, phone number, or message.
3. Use filters:
   - All
   - New
   - Contacted
   - Closed
4. Use quick actions:
   - Mark as contacted
   - Mark as closed
   - Open WhatsApp
   - Delete enquiry

## Reply On WhatsApp

1. Open `Enquiries`.
2. Click `Open WhatsApp`.
3. WhatsApp opens with a prepared message.
4. Send the message to the customer.

## Logout

Click `Logout` in the admin sidebar when finished.

## Important Notes

- Do not share your admin password.
- Keep product prices and stock updated.
- Hide products that are not ready for customers.
- Check enquiries regularly.
